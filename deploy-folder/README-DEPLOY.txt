================================================================
BUSINESS EMPIRE - DEPLOYMENT INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================

Your app is built and ready to deploy! Here are 3 easy ways to get your APK:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ METHOD 1: Netlify Drag & Drop (EASIEST - 2 minutes)         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Open https://www.netlify.com/ in your browser
2. Click "Sign up" (free) or "Log in" if you have an account
3. On the dashboard, go to "Sites" tab
4. DRAG this entire "dist" folder onto the page:
   Location: D:\business\game\Business-Empire\dist
5. Wait for upload to complete (30 seconds)
6. Netlify will give you a URL like:
   https://business-empire-abc123.netlify.app
7. COPY that URL

Then go to: https://www.pwabuilder.com/
→ Paste the URL
→ Click "Package for Android"
→ Download APK

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ METHOD 2: GitHub Pages (Free, No Signup Needed for PWA)    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Go to https://github.com/ and create a free account
2. Click "+" → "New repository"
3. Name it: business-empire
4. Click "Create repository"
5. Click "uploading an existing file"
6. Upload ALL files from the "dist" folder:
   D:\business\game\Business-Empire\dist\*
7. Click "Commit changes"
8. Go to Settings → Pages
9. Under "Source", select "main" branch
10. Click Save
11. Wait 1-2 minutes
12. Your URL will be: https://yourusername.github.io/business-empire
13. COPY that URL

Then go to: https://www.pwabuilder.com/
→ Paste the URL
→ Click "Package for Android"
→ Download APK

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ METHOD 3: Use Vercel (Fastest if you have account)         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Open https://vercel.com/ in your browser
2. Click "Sign up" or "Log in"
3. Click "Add New Project"
4. Import from Git (or upload)
5. Connect this folder: D:\business\game\Business-Empire
6. Click "Deploy"
7. Vercel will give you a URL
8. COPY that URL

Then go to: https://www.pwabuilder.com/
→ Paste the URL
→ Click "Package for Android"
→ Download APK

================================================================
AFTER YOU GET THE URL
================================================================

Go to: https://www.pwabuilder.com/

Steps:
1. Click "Start" or "Package for Web"
2. Paste your deployed URL (from Netlify/GitHub/Vercel)
3. Click "Next" or "Analyze"
4. Wait for analysis (10 seconds)
5. Click "Package for Android"
6. Click "Download APK"
7. APK file will download to your computer

================================================================
INSTALL ON YOUR PHONE
================================================================

1. Transfer the APK file to your Android phone
   - Via USB cable
   - Or email it to yourself
   - Or use Google Drive/Dropbox

2. On your phone:
   - Go to Settings → Security
   - Enable "Install from Unknown Sources"
   - Find the APK file in your Downloads
   - Tap to install
   - Open "Business Empire" app!
